## 2026-10-22

### Futures V3 & Testnet — Get Income History With Pagination

* Added `GET /fapi/v3/incomePage` (USER_DATA, weight 30): paginated income history with `symbol`, `incomeType`, `startTime`, `endTime`, `page` (default 1), and `limit` (default 100; max 1000) filters.
* Response: `total`, `page`, `limit`, `hasMore`, `rows[]` (same fields as `GET /fapi/v3/income`).
* No limit on the time span; `page` × `limit` ≤ 100,000. Defaults to the recent 7 days if neither `startTime` nor `endTime` is sent.
* Requires V3 authentication parameters: `nonce`, `signer`, `signature`.
